Bodes Health – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Bodes Health is a personal app used only by its developer.

What it reads

With your permission, the app reads your health and fitness data from Google Health (Fitbit), COROS and Garmin, using read-only access. It never writes, changes or deletes data in those services.

Where data is kept

Everything the app downloads is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to any server, shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising or analytics. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain.

Google user data

Data received from Google APIs is used only to show it to you inside the app, and is not transferred to anyone. The app's use of that data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes all its data from the phone. You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps), in the COROS app, or in Garmin Connect.
